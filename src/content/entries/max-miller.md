## Overview

Max Miller is a Republican U.S. representative from Ohio. On August 4, 2026, the House Committee on Ethics opened an investigation into whether Miller may have engaged in domestic violence and abuse or illegal drug use. Miller requested the investigation and denied the allegations.

## Description

The committee's action is an investigation, not a finding that misconduct occurred. The Associated Press reported that Miller's former wife, Emily Moreno, made allegations and that Miller remained in the 2026 reelection contest. Miller said he had nothing to hide and wanted the investigation to clear his name. This entry records the public allegations and official inquiry without treating them as adjudicated facts.
