## Overview

On August 3, 2026, the U.S. House Committee on Ethics recommended that the House censure Representative Chuck Edwards after finding persistent unprofessional and inappropriate conduct toward two young female aides. The committee report and contemporaneous reporting describe conduct including comments about appearance and dress, gifts, private dinners or trips, and affectionate notes.

## Description

Edwards denied sexual or romantic intent. The Associated Press reported that the committee found no evidence of an explicit proposition or sexual activity, while concluding that a reasonable observer could interpret the pattern of conduct as sexual or romantic. The full House must vote on the recommended censure. Edwards withdrew his reelection bid on August 5 and said he would complete his current term.

## August 2026 Update

This entry was reviewed against the House Ethics Committee report and August 3 and August 5 Associated Press coverage. It records the committee's findings and Edwards's response; it does not treat the reported conduct as a criminal conviction.
